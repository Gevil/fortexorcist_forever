# Mage live check: generated `Mage.lua` vs the live client (70790 store, 70862 DB2), Blizzard notes Nov 6-10

Researched 2026-11-09 by RsrchMage (read-only; the only file written is this one; scratch work in `/tmp/rsm`).
Scope: `tools/spell_lists/mage.json`, `addon/Forte_Mage/Data/Mage.lua`. Skipped on purpose (already known, see
`docs/research/data-diff-live-2026-11-07.md` §5 and `forever-postlaunch-2026-11-07.md` §0): the Arcane Blast 18091 vs 400573
story and the 70840 NPC-row warning in general. I re-verified those two claims and add what the earlier notes did not cover.

**Labels.** `[V]` verified: quote or value from a page/file I fetched (URL or file:line given). `[D]` derived: I joined or
computed it from fetched data (method given). `[I]` inference: my reading, nobody tested it. `[UNVERIFIED]`: could not be checked.

## 0. Answer first (ranked by what breaks)

1. **[D] The generated Fire Blast row is the wrong spell.** `Mage.lua:94` = `400623` (cd 15 s, no GCD). That id is the
   Season-of-Discovery "Overheat" action-bar stand-in, not the Rank 7 the player trains. The trainable Rank 7 is `10199`:
   8 s cooldown, 1.5 s GCD, category 19. The generator picked 400623 because 10199 and 400623 tie on level (54) and the rank law
   then takes the higher id. **Fix: pin `Fire Blast` to 10199** (§3.1, patch P1). Confidence high.
2. **[D] `Arcane Blast = 400573` is right, but the timer will draw it wrong.** 400573 is the 18 s, 4-stack aura (re-checked,
   §2.1). The window does **not** refresh on a recast, but `Rows:InsertFromCast` removes and re-inserts the bar on every cast
   (`Forte_Timer/Core/Rows.lua:261-326`). After the second cast the bar overstates the remaining time by the gap between casts.
   A second pair of item procs is also named "Arcane Blast" (18091 Archeus **and 20883 Joonho's Mercy**, §3.3). Needs a
   "keep first window" law, not a data change.
3. **[D] Frost Armor 1283419 is a decoy; the generated row is correct (7301).** Evidence in §3.2. A defensive pin is cheap.
4. **[D] About 15 tracked mage names are Season-of-Discovery rune spells (or their companions) that the 70862 client data
   does not make learnable** (no trainer/talent row, or `AcquireMethod 3`). Neither Blizzard's Mage class write-up nor a
   third-party 70862 spellbook lists them. §4 has the table; the removal is **medium** confidence until one in-game
   `C_SpellBook.IsSpellKnown` probe (§9) confirms it.
5. **[D] Every other cooldown/duration you asked about is correct in `Mage.lua`** (table in §2). Two behaviours the bar gets
   wrong, though: **Combustion** and **Presence of Mind** start their cooldown when the effect ends, not at the cast
   (§2.5), and **Cold Snap** resets other Frost cooldowns, which the engine does not model (§2.6).
6. **[D] The Ice Lance / Fire Blast shared 8 s cooldown (dev notes post 9) is not wired.** Both are on category 19, but
   `mage.json` has no `_shares` entry, so a cast of one does not start the other's bar (§2.4, patch P4).
7. **[D] Presence of Mind (12043) is not tracked.** It resolves with 180 s, category 1151 (shared with Combustion). Patch P3.
8. **[V] Blizzard Nov 6-10:** one Mage line set (Nov 6 hotfix: Arcane Blast duration 18 s; Research/Study spell). The Nov 10
   class-tuning post names no Mage change. No newer class post exists through 2026-11-09 21:10 UTC (§7).

### 2.4 Ice Lance / Fire Blast share one bar

[D] `SpellCategories` has category 19 on Fire Blast 2136-10199 (and the 4006xx copies) and, per the sim's own comment, on Ice
Lance too ("Its 8 sec cooldown runs on category 19, which Fire Blast shares, so casting either puts both on cooldown",
`sim/mage/ice_lance.go` doc comment). The engine can do this (`Engine:OnCast` partner walk, `Forte_Cooldown/Core/Engine.lua:
~226-262`, fed by `shares = "<partner>"` rows, generator `_shares`, `gen_spell_data.py:80-82,714-718,941-943`). `mage.json` has no
`_shares` key (the Hunter list has one for Summon Hawk/Arcane Shot), and `Mage.lua:94-95` carries no `shares` field. So the
dev-notes action item "Mage Ice Lance/Fire Blast shared category on the cooldown bar" (`docs/research/forever-dev-notes-
2026-11-03.md` §3.2) is **not done for the Mage**. In the /tmp generator copy, `"_shares": {"Ice Lance": "Fire Blast"}`
emits `shares = "Fire Blast"` on the Ice Lance row and the file loads in luajit.

Attr0 bit `0x2000000` is set on 11129 and 12043 and not on Arcane Power. The sim authors read it as "Disabled While Active:
the cooldown starts when the effect ends / the charge is spent" (`sim/mage/combustion.go` and `presence_of_mind.go` comments)
[V as quoted code comments, not measured by me]. `Engine:OnCast` starts a predicted bar of `BestDuration` from the cast instant
(`Engine.lua:~244-247`). **[I]** Combustion's bar would therefore finish early by the time it takes to spend 3 crits, and PoM's
by the time until the next cast. A learned duration does not fix that (it learns the 180 s, not the offset). Check after one
real cast (§9).

### 2.6 Cold Snap resets Frost cooldowns

S6 `cold_snap.go`: "Finishes the remaining cooldown on all your other Frost spells." The sim resets Frost Nova, Cone of Cold,
Ice Block and Ice Barrier, and deliberately not Frost Ward (shares category 56 with Fire Ward). Our engine has no reset concept
(`Cold Snap` appears only as a kind-rule regex, `Forte_Timer/Core/Registry.lua:187`). **[I]** After a Cold Snap in combat the
Frost Nova / Cone of Cold / Ice Block / Ice Barrier bars keep running until a plain read can re-anchor them. This is the Mage
twin of the Hunter trap-category work.
